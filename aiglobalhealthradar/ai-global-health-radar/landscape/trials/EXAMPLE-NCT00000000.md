## Summary

A fictional prospective study testing whether ExampleDx's AI chest-X-ray triage tool improves tuberculosis case-finding when run by frontline health workers in Kenyan primary-care clinics [ct-001]. Eligible adults presenting with respiratory symptoms receive an X-ray that the software scores for TB abnormality; all participants also receive molecular confirmatory testing as the reference standard [ct-001]. The AI component is the triage decision: which patients the model flags for confirmatory testing [ct-001]. The primary measure is diagnostic accuracy against molecular reference; a secondary measure is the share of confirmed cases the tool would have routed to testing under a frontline-worker workflow [ct-001]. It matters because it would move ExampleDx from retrospective real-world evidence [pm-001] toward prospective, workflow-embedded validation in an LMIC primary-care setting.
