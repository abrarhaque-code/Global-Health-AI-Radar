## Promotion criteria

A watchlist row becomes a full card when at least one of the following is true and the two gates are met:

- A priced equity round closes with a named institutional investor.
- A regulatory milestone is reached in a priority market.
- An LMIC deployment or named partnership is documented with a citation.
- A signal trigger from `taxonomy/signal_triggers.yaml` fires on the entity.
